##  About SpaceOra

SpaceOra is a mobile platform designed to make renting spaces simple,
secure, and convenient.

It connects renters looking for suitable spaces with owners who want
to list and manage their available spaces.

---

## ✨ What Can You Rent?

| 🚗 Parking | 📦 Storage | 🏠 Basements | ✨ More |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Find parking spaces | Store your belongings | Rent available spaces | Discover unique spaces |

---

## ⭐ Key Features

- 🔐 Login & Sign Up
- 👥 Renter and Owner Role Selection
- 🔎 Browse Available Spaces
- 🏠 Add and Manage Spaces
- 📸 Space Images
- 📍 Space Details
- 📅 Booking System
- ✅ Owner Verification
- 💳 Payment Management
- ⭐ Reviews & Ratings
- 🗄️ Supabase Database

# 🗄️ Database

SpaceOra uses **Supabase with PostgreSQL** to store and manage application data.

### Main Tables

- 👤 Profiles
- 🏠 Spaces
- 📸 Space Images
- ✅ Owner Verification
- 📅 Bookings
- 💳 Payments
- ⭐ Reviews
